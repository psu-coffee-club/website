Our kind
of field trip.

We turn State College into a tasting room. Past crawls have teamed up with local cafés for club-only menus and a drink to try at each stop.

Previous crawl stops have included Sowers Harvest Café, Vibe Coffee Co., and Webster’s Bookstore Café. Routes can change each semester.
